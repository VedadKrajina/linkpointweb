Cookie Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026  ·  Effective: 10 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how LinkPoint Solutions d.o.o. uses cookies and similar technologies on linkpointsolutions.ba. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy. By using our website, you agree to our use of essential cookies. For non-essential cookies, we ask for your consent via our cookie banner.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your device on future visits and remember certain information — such as your login status or preferences.

Similar technologies include localStorage (used by our chat widget to remember session tokens), sessionStorage, and server-side session identifiers stored in HTTP cookies. This policy covers all such technologies.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential operation — to keep you logged in, protect your session, and ensure the platform functions correctly.
  • Preference memory — to remember your cookie consent choice so we don't ask repeatedly.
  • Analytics — to understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it. We use aggregate, anonymised data for this purpose.
  • Performance — to identify and resolve technical issues.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the website and platform to function. They cannot be disabled without breaking core functionality. No consent is required for essential cookies under applicable law.

Cookie / Key Purpose Duration Type
lp_session Authenticated session token for platform users (agents, admins, partner accounts). Stored in an httpOnly cookie to prevent JavaScript access. Session (browser close) Essential
lp_consent Records your cookie consent choice (accepted / declined) so the banner is not shown on every visit. 1 year Essential (functional)
lp_widget_[token] Stores the anonymous conversation session token for the embedded chat widget (localStorage on partner websites). Scoped to each partner's embed token. Session / 30 days Essential

3.2 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website. All data is pseudonymised and aggregated. They are only set if you accept cookies via our banner.

Cookie Provider Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes unique visitors. Used to calculate visit, session, and campaign data for site traffic reports. 2 years
_ga_[ID] Google Analytics (GA4) Stores and counts page views. 2 years
_gid Google Analytics Distinguishes users over short periods; used in session sampling. 24 hours

Google Analytics data is processed by Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Data may be transferred to Google servers in the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses. For Google's privacy practices, see policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of Google Analytics across all websites at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

3.3 Marketing & Remarketing Cookies

We do not currently use marketing or remarketing cookies. If this changes, this policy will be updated and your consent will be sought before any such cookies are placed.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Certain third-party services integrated into our platform may set their own cookies. These are governed by the respective third parties' privacy policies and are not under our control:

  • Meta / WhatsApp — if you interact with our WhatsApp Business channel, Meta may set cookies on their platform. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Vapi.ai — used for AI phone answering (where applicable). Vapi processes audio transcripts on our behalf.

Our embedded chat widget is served in a sandboxed cross-origin iframe. It does not have access to cookies or data on your website's domain. Communication between the widget and the host page is restricted to postMessage with strict origin validation.

5. Your Choices & How to Control Cookies

5.1 Cookie Banner

When you first visit our website, a cookie banner appears at the bottom of the screen. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies. Your choice is saved for 1 year. You may change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage for our domain, which will re-display the banner.

5.2 Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete them. To do so, refer to your browser's help documentation:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Apple Safari

Please note: disabling essential cookies may prevent you from logging in or using the platform. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect your ability to use the website.

5.3 Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. We honour the DNT signal by not activating analytics cookies when it is detected.

6. Legal Basis (Bosnia and Herzegovina)

Bosnia and Herzegovina does not yet have a dedicated e-Privacy / cookie directive. As an EU candidate country, we voluntarily align our cookie practices with the EU ePrivacy Directive and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) standards:

  • Essential cookies: legitimate interests / necessity for service — no consent required.
  • Analytics cookies: consent — only placed after you accept via our cookie banner.

The processing of any personal data contained within cookies is also governed by the Law on Protection of Personal Data of Bosnia and Herzegovina (Official Gazette 49/06, 76/11, 89/11). See our Privacy Policy for full details of your rights.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when we add or remove cookies, or when the law requires it. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent changes. Significant changes will be communicated via a renewed consent banner.
