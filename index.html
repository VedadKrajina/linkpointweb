FAQ

Common questions

How does your AI handle customer questions? +

We use Anthropic's Claude model, trained on your uploaded knowledge base — your FAQs, product documentation, and common technical problems with solutions. When a customer sends a message, Claude retrieves relevant context, drafts a reply, and applies a confidence threshold. High-confidence answers are sent automatically; low-confidence queries are escalated to a human agent.

Can the AI be switched off for specific clients? +

Yes. Every partner company has an individual AI toggle on the platform. Admins can enable or disable AI at any time without affecting other partners. When AI is off, all conversations route directly to human agents.

What happens when AI can't resolve a query? +

The AI informs the customer politely that a specialist will follow up, then creates a ticket in our agent inbox. The assigned human agent receives full conversation context and can continue the conversation or — if it was a phone call — schedule a callback during business hours.

How does the WhatsApp integration work? +

We connect to the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API using your verified Business Account. Inbound customer messages flow into the same unified inbox as web chat. Service conversations (customer-initiated within 24 hours) are free on Meta's infrastructure. Template messages (outbound notifications) are charged at Meta's standard rates per category and country.

Is customer data secure and compliant? +

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. We operate under the Law on Protection of Personal Data of Bosnia and Herzegovina (Official Gazette 49/06, 76/11, 89/11), which follows EU Directive 95/46/EC principles. The platform uses cross-origin sandboxed iframes with no allow-same-origin so your customers' conversation data cannot be accessed by the host page.

How long does onboarding take? +

For most clients, the platform is live within one business day. We create your partner profile, ingest your knowledge base, and generate your embed snippet — which your team pastes into your website's HTML. WhatsApp and phone integrations require Meta Business verification and Vapi configuration, which typically adds 3–5 business days.

Are there long-term contracts? +

No. All plans are billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time with 30 days' notice. Enterprise clients may negotiate annual pricing for a discount. There are no setup fees and no lock-in penalties.
