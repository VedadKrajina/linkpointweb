Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026  ·  Effective: 10 September 2026

This Privacy Policy is issued in accordance with the Law on Protection of Personal Data of Bosnia and Herzegovina (Zakon o zaštiti ličnih podataka BiH, Official Gazette of BiH 49/06, 76/11 and 89/11) and applicable international data protection standards. Your rights and our obligations are outlined below in plain language.

1. Who We Are

LinkPoint Solutions d.o.o. ("LinkPoint", "we", "us", "our") is a limited liability company registered in Bosnia and Herzegovina, with its registered office at Ferhadija 1, 71000 Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina.

LinkPoint acts as the Data Controller in respect of personal data collected through this website (linkpointsolutions.ba) and our BPO platform. For enquiries about this policy, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@linkpointsolutions.ba
  • Post: Ferhadija 1, 71000 Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Information you provide to us

  • Contact information — name, email address, telephone number, company name, when you submit an enquiry or start a trial.
  • Account credentials — username and hashed password for platform accounts created by our administrative staff.
  • Communications — content of messages you send to us via email, web forms, or our platform's internal messaging.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, timestamps, session duration, and navigation paths.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, referrer URL.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — see our Cookie Policy for full details.

2.3 Customer support data (Partner clients)

When you or your customers interact with our embedded chat widget, WhatsApp channel, or AI phone answering service on behalf of a partner client:

  • Chat transcripts, message content, and AI/human agent replies.
  • Phone call transcripts and call recordings (where applicable).
  • Conversation flags and categorisation metadata.
  • Customer contact information provided voluntarily during the conversation.

In these cases, LinkPoint acts as a Data Processor on behalf of the partner company, which is the Data Controller for their end customers' data. Our data processing agreement with each partner governs this relationship.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases (Article 5, BiH ZZLP):

  • Performance of a contract — to provide our platform services and fulfil obligations under our service agreements with partner clients.
  • Legitimate interests — to operate, improve, and secure our platform, prevent fraud, and maintain service quality, provided our interests do not override your fundamental rights.
  • Your consent — for analytics cookies, marketing communications, and any processing you have specifically agreed to. You may withdraw consent at any time (see Your Rights, section 7).
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required by applicable law.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Providing and managing our BPO and customer support platform.
  • Responding to enquiries, support requests, and demonstrations.
  • Sending service updates, invoices, and essential platform notifications.
  • Analysing usage patterns to improve platform performance and features.
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, security threats, and abuse.
  • Complying with legal obligations and resolving disputes.
  • Marketing our services to prospective and existing clients — only where you have provided consent or where we have a legitimate interest and you have not opted out.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described:

  • Account data: retained for the duration of the contractual relationship plus 3 years after termination, unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Chat transcripts & call recordings: retained for 12 months from the date of conversation, or as agreed with the partner client.
  • Marketing data: retained until you withdraw consent or opt out.
  • Website usage / analytics data: aggregated and anonymised after 13 months.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with trusted third parties only where necessary:

  • Infrastructure providers — cloud hosting (Vercel, Neon/Supabase) operating under appropriate data processing agreements.
  • AI service provider — Anthropic, Inc. (United States), for AI response generation. Data is not used by Anthropic to train their models under our API agreement.
  • Telephony — Vapi.ai (where phone support is activated), for speech-to-text transcription.
  • Messaging — Meta Platforms, Inc., for WhatsApp Business API integration (where activated by a partner client).
  • Analytics — as described in our Cookie Policy.
  • Legal authorities — where required by applicable law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of individuals.

7. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers are located outside Bosnia and Herzegovina and the European Economic Area (EEA). Where personal data is transferred internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission.
  • Data processing agreements that mandate equivalent protection to that provided under BiH and EU law.

8. Your Rights

Under the Law on Protection of Personal Data of Bosnia and Herzegovina you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 20 ZZLP): to obtain confirmation of whether we process your data, and a copy of it.
  • Right to correction (Art. 21 ZZLP): to have inaccurate data corrected without undue delay.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 22 ZZLP): to request deletion of your data where it is no longer necessary, you withdraw consent, or processing is unlawful.
  • Right to restrict processing (Art. 23 ZZLP): to request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to object (Art. 24 ZZLP): to object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to object to direct marketing at any time.
  • Right to data portability: to receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@linkpointsolutions.ba. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • TLS 1.2+ encryption for all data in transit.
  • Encryption at rest for database storage.
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) limiting data access to authorised personnel only.
  • Cross-origin isolated iframe embeds with no allow-same-origin attribute, preventing host-page access to customer conversation data.
  • Audit logging of all administrative actions.
  • Regular security reviews of our infrastructure.

In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the supervisory authority and, where applicable, affected individuals as required by law.

10. Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies. For detailed information on the cookies we use, their purposes, and how to control them, please read our Cookie Policy.

11. Children's Data

Our platform and services are intended for business use and are not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the most recent changes were made. For material changes, we will provide notice via email or a prominent notice on our website at least 14 days before the changes take effect.
